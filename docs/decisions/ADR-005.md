# ADR-005: Blocks to the owner say the exact instant to act

Date: 2026-10-15
Status: Accepted

## Context

The owner is the only relay between agents (ADR-004). Blocks to the owner said "merge" in every step
but never said when: before relaying or after, now or later. The owner had to guess, and asked for the
instant to be made clear in the skill.

## Decision

- Every numbered line in a block `To owner` starts with a time word: `Now:`, `After <event>:`, or `Not yet:`.
- Every block to the owner has exactly one `Merge:` line: `now`, `not yet`, or `nothing to merge`.
- The merge moment is fixed: exactly once, right after the reviewer's approve block, before any other relay.
  Written once as "The PR loop for the owner" in `docs/TEAM_PROTOCOL.md`.
- `scripts/check_block.py` enforces the two rules for owner blocks. Other roles are unchanged.

## Consequences

Good:

- The owner never guesses. Every line says now, later, or not yet.
- One table answers "when do I merge" for every PR.
- A vague owner block fails in CI when it sits in a PR body.

Bad and to watch:

- Review bodies are not checked by CI, so reviewer-to-owner blocks rely on the skill text.
- `After <event>:` is free text. The skill says the event must be a block, a CI result, or a sha.

## Rollback

- Remove the two owner checks from `scripts/check_block.py` and their tests. Keep the table.
